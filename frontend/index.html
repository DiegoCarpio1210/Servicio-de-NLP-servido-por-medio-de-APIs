About this evaluation

Exact Match measures normalized answer agreement. F1 measures word overlap. These quality40k results use the length05 policy on 1,024 reserved examples. The app uses quality40k checkpoint-2000; continued20k is retained as backup. The reserve comes from the training split, with uncertain historical exposure from an aborted run. It is not the instructor’s official test.

An answer score is a model signal, not a calibrated probability of correctness. The model may return no answer when the information is insufficient.
